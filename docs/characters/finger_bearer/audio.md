# Finger Bearer / Explosive Energy — Audio & Sound Design

## 1. Sound Assets & Events
- **Bedrock references**:
  - Guttural curse laughter and screeching.
  - High-gain explosion sound triggers.
  - Energy charge whine.
- **Java SoundEvent Registry Entries**:
  - `crb:finger_bearer.ambient`: Unsettling wet breathing and disjointed clicks.
  - `crb:finger_bearer.giggle`: Distorted infant giggle mocking the target.
  - `crb:finger_bearer.scream`: Ear-piercing screech during Mugetsu beam charge.
  - `crb:finger_bearer.energy_blast`: Heavy thunderous boom of raw CE detonation.
  - `crb:finger_bearer.death`: Dissolving hiss and dying gurgle as flesh turns into cursed vapor.
