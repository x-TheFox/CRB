# Finger Bearer / Explosive Energy — Domain Profile & Innate Domain

## 1. Domain Status
- **Canon Context**: Finger Bearers are cursed wobs that have not developed an innate cursed technique; therefore, they **cannot manifest a complete Domain Expansion with a barrier and sure-hit**.
- **Incomplete Innate Domain (生得領域の展開)**:
  - *Canon ref*: Chapter 6 (Epon Detention Center). The juvenile detention center interior was distorted into a massive labyrinthine cavern due to the Finger Bearer's subconscious innate domain manifesting over the physical building without a barrier.
  - *JJA / CRB Mechanics*:
    - Finger Bearers do not possess an active Domain Expansion ability key.
    - However, world structures inhabited by Finger Bearers (e.g. Detention Centers, Under-Bridge Caves) generate with corrupted geometry, endless staircases, and ominous purple ambient lighting representing their unclosed innate domain.
