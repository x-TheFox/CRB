# Finger Bearer / Explosive Energy — Canon Upgrades (§5.4)

## 1. Canon Abilities Missing or Truncated in JJA
1. **Raw CE Regeneration (呪霊の瞬時再生)**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 7. When Yuji cuts off the Finger Bearer's arms, the cursed spirit regenerates them instantly without Reverse Cursed Technique (since cursed spirits are composed entirely of cursed energy and can reconstruct themselves simply by outputting more CE).
   - *JJA omission*: Handled via basic Minecraft health potions without visual limb regeneration.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Rapid spirit regeneration capability: if not struck by positive energy (RCT) or soul damage, regenerates 3 hearts/sec and rapidly regrows severed visual body segments.

2. **Innate Domain Spatial Labyrinth Generation**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 6. Entering the detention center creates a non-Euclidean space where the interior is 10 times larger than the exterior building, with floating stairways and bottomless chasms.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Dedicated world generation dungeon with shifting corridors and inverted physics when approaching a dormant Finger Bearer.

3. **Consuming Multiple Fingers (Evolution Trigger)**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 58. The Yasohachi Bridge Finger Bearer had absorbed a finger much earlier and displayed greater intelligence, speed, and refined cursed energy control than the detention center variant.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Boss evolution mechanic: feeding additional fingers to a Finger Bearer increases its stats, size, and unlocks new beam variants.
