## 2. Canon Lore Summary
A Finger Bearer is a Special Grade cursed spirit born when a cursed womb incarnates by assimilating one of Sukuna's twenty indestructible fingers. Because they have not existed long enough to develop an innate cursed technique, they fight by weaponizing colossal reserves of raw, unrefined cursed energy. Their strikes detonate with catastrophic explosive force, blasting limbs and blowing open reinforced concrete structures with pure kinetic bursts, energy arrows, and raw palm cannons.
