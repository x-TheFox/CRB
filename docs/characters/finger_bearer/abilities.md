# Finger Bearer / Explosive Energy — Abilities & Kit Specification

## 1. Raw Palm Blast (掌底呪力砲)
- **Canon ref**: Chapters 6–8 (Detention Center vs Megumi & Yuji)
- **JJA implementation**: `palm_blast.mcfunction`, `explosive1.mcfunction`.
- **Cost / Cooldown / Range**:
  - Cost: 40 CE.
  - Cooldown: 4 seconds.
  - Range: 18 blocks.
- **Damage / Effect**:
  - Blasts a high-pressure cone of raw purple/blue cursed energy from the open palm.
  - Deals 20 explosive damage, creates block debris, and knocks back targets 12 blocks.

---

## 2. Ground Slam & Seismic Eruption (大地粉砕)
- **Canon ref**: Chapter 7
- **JJA implementation**: `ground_slam.mcfunction`, `shockwave.mcfunction`, `explosive2.mcfunction`.
- **Cost / Cooldown / Range**:
  - Cost: 55 CE.
  - Cooldown: 7 seconds.
  - Range: 8-block radial crater.
- **Damage / Effect**:
  - Slams both fists into bedrock, causing a violent seismic eruption.
  - Deals 26 physical/explosive damage and launches caught victims into the air.

---

## 3. Finger Arrow (呪力矢 / 遠距離狙撃)
- **Canon ref**: Chapter 58 (Yasohachi Bridge vs Megumi)
- **JJA implementation**: `finger_arrow.mcfunction`, `arrow.mcfunction`, `bolt.mcfunction`, `explosive3.mcfunction`.
- **Cost / Cooldown / Range**:
  - Cost: 35 CE.
  - Cooldown: 3 seconds.
  - Range: 45 blocks.
- **Damage / Effect**:
  - Points index finger and discharges a concentrated needle-thin supersonic projectile of pure CE.
  - Deals 18 piercing damage and penetrates through multiple entities in a row.

---

## 4. Teleporting Ambush Punch (瞬間移動殴打)
- **Canon ref**: Chapter 6
- **JJA implementation**: `tp_punch.mcfunction`, `explosive5.mcfunction`.
- **Cost / Cooldown / Range**:
  - Cost: 65 CE.
  - Cooldown: 9 seconds.
  - Range: 22 blocks target lock.
- **Damage / Effect**:
  - Instantly teleports directly behind the target and uncorks a vicious roundhouse right hook.
  - Deals 22 damage and stuns victim for 20 ticks.

---

## 5. Mugetsu: Condensed Energy Annihilation (無月・凝縮呪力砲)
- **Canon ref**: Chapter 58 (Final clash against Megumi's Divine Dog)
- **JJA implementation**: `mugetsu.mcfunction`, `mugetsu2.mcfunction`, `explosive6.mcfunction`.
- **Cost / Cooldown / Range**:
  - Cost: 140 CE.
  - Cooldown: 25 seconds.
  - Range: 35 blocks line.
- **Damage / Effect**:
  - Charges a super-dense pulsating black/purple sphere of cursed energy at the mouth or chest.
  - Fires a continuous beam for 20 ticks dealing 8 damage per tick (total 80 hearts maximum potential).
  - Destroys soft blocks along the beam corridor.
