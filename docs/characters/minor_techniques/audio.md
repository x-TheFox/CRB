# Minor Cursed Techniques — Audio & Sound Design

## 1. Sound Assets & Events
- **Sound Events**:
  - `crb:miracle.save_ding`: Soft playful casino bell when a lethal blow is miraculously avoided.
  - `crb:charles.ink_splat`: Wet brush stroke sound upon applying ink.
  - `crb:charles.manga_page_flip`: Rapid paper turning sound when the future manga panel updates.
  - `crb:painkiller.numb_hum`: Muffled, low-pass underwater audio filter applied while under anesthesia.
  - `crb:sight.stare_drone`: Ominous high-pitch tinnitus whistle during sustained eye contact.
