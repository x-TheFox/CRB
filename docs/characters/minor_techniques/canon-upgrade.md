# Minor Cursed Techniques — Canon Upgrades (§5.4)

## 1. Upgrades to Minor Techniques
1. **Haruta Shigemo's Flexible Hand Sword**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 100. Haruta's sword pommel is shaped like a human hand that autonomously flexes, grips his wrist, and alters the swing trajectory mid-slash.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Dynamic weapon physics: Attacks have variable reach (3 to 5 blocks) and can curve around enemy shields.

2. **Charles Bernard: Multiple Panel Depth**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 182. Landing multiple hits deposits more ink, allowing Charles to see 2 seconds, 3 seconds, and up to 5 seconds into the future, creating multiple layered comic frames.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Consecutive ink strikes spawn up to 3 sequential future panels, increasing dodge time and critical strike chance against the marked target.
