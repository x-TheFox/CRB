# Character Dossier: Minor Cursed Techniques (準一級・補助術式集)

## 1. Identity & Roster Profile
This dossier encompasses the 4 auxiliary cursed techniques present in the JJA addon under `functions/small_cts/`:
1. **Miracles (奇跡, Kiseki)** — Haruta Shigemo (重面 春太)
2. **G Warstaff / Future Vision (G戦杖 / 漫画のコマ)** — Charles Bernard (シャルル・ベルナール)
3. **Pain-Killer (痛覚遮断)** — Supportive Anesthetic Sorcery
4. **Immobilizing Sight (呪視 / 凝視拘束)** — Visual Line-of-Sight Paralysis

- **JJA Internal Namespace / Folders**: `functions/small_cts/`, `items/small_cts/`

## 2. Canon Lore Summary
In the Jujutsu world, not every sorcerer possesses cataclysmic Special Grade techniques like Limitless or Malevolent Shrine. A variety of highly specialized, idiosyncratic innate techniques exist:
- **Haruta Shigemo's Miracles**: Subconsciously erases memories of everyday small miracles (e.g. clock showing consecutive numbers, finding a coin) and stores them as marks beneath his eyes. When receiving an otherwise fatal wound, a stored miracle is automatically spent to convert the mortal injury into a minor scratch.
- **Charles Bernard's G Warstaff**: A large spear shaped like a manga artist's G-pen nib. By drawing blood and imbuing the victim's chest with ink, a manga panel manifests in mid-air projecting the opponent's actions 1 to 2 seconds into the future.
- **Pain-Killer & Sight**: Auxiliary cursed energy manipulation allowing sorcerers to numb nervous systems or freeze opponents through ocular cursed energy reinforcement.
