# Kinji Hakari — Canon Upgrades (§5.4)

## 1. Canon Abilities Missing or Truncated in JJA
1. **Binding Vow: Sacrificial Arm Severance**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 190 (Hakari vs Kashimo). As Kashimo's steam explosion detonates, Hakari makes an instantaneous binding vow: he sacrifices his left arm without reinforcing it, channeling all defensive cursed energy into the rest of his body to survive the explosion point-blank.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Emergency Defensive Vow Key: sacrifices left arm (inflicts severed limb state) to grant complete invulnerability for 20 ticks against a single lethal blast.

2. **Domain Coordinate Relocation (結界の座標移動)**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 189. Hakari shifts the external coordinate boundaries of his domain while inside it, moving the domain several dozen blocks across the map so that when it breaks, he and Kashimo drop directly into the ocean.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Domain Master Feature: While inside Idle Death Gamble, Hakari can steer the barrier anchor vector up to 40 blocks in horizontal directions.

3. **Consecutive Immediate Domain Recast**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 187, 189. The overflowing cursed energy from a Jackpot mode naturally heals Hakari's burnt-out cursed technique! The instant the 4m11s music ends, Hakari can cast Idle Death Gamble without waiting for burnout cooldown.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Automated CT Burnout Purge: At tick 5,019 of Jackpot mode, CT burnout timer is set to 0.

4. **Nose Blast RCT Cleansing (塩素ガス強制排出)**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 189. When poisoned by chlorine gas, Hakari's automatic RCT reflexively blasts the toxic chemicals out through his nostrils.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Automatic negative potion cleanse with steam jet particle burst from the face.
