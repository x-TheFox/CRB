# Kinji Hakari — Domain Expansion: Idle Death Gamble (坐殺博徒)

## 1. Domain Architecture & The Pachinko Machine
- **Barrier Type**: Enclosed 48-block spherical domain barrier with bright neon signage, subway station tiles, and railway tracks across the ceiling.
- **Rules Infusion Sure-Hit**:
  - The moment the barrier manifests, the target is struck with an instantaneous mental transmission explaining the entire ruleset of *Private Pure Love Train*.
  - Because this sure-hit has zero offensive capability, it forms faster than lethal domains (defeating standard domain clashes in speed).

## 2. Riichi Scenarios & Odds Evaluation
- **Slot Reel Symbols (1 through 7)**:
  - Odd numbers (1, 3, 5, 7): High-tier Jackpot (Guarantees faster spin times in subsequent domain).
  - Even numbers (2, 4, 6): Standard Jackpot.
  - Number 7: Love Train Gold Rush!
- **Visual Color Indicators**:
  - Green balls / green doors: 15% probability.
  - Red balls / red doors: 45% probability.
  - Golden balls / rainbow doors: 95% probability.
- **Cinematic Riichi Drama**:
  - Inside the domain, characters from *Private Pure Love Train* (Yume-chan and the protagonist) appear in dramatic scenes.
  - If the scene succeeds (e.g. they catch the last train together), the reels line up 7-7-7.
  - The domain shatters into iridescent golden glass shards, and Hakari enters Jackpot mode.
