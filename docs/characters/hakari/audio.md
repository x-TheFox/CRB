# Kinji Hakari — Audio & Sound Design

## 1. Sound Assets & Events
- **Bedrock references**:
  - `music.tukadonka`: The high-energy jackpot romance anime / phonk soundtrack playing continuously during Jackpot.
  - `mob.deathgamble1`: Domain activation chime and pachinko reel spin.
  - `mob.bass`: Sub-bass drop on domain boundary formation.
- **Java SoundEvent Registry Entries**:
  - `crb:hakari.jackpot_music`: High-fidelity stereo broadcast of the theme song (*Admiring You* / *Tuka Donka*), synchronized between all clients within earshot.
  - `crb:hakari.shutter_slam`: Heavy hydraulic pneumatic hiss followed by metallic crash.
  - `crb:hakari.pachinko_spin`: Mechanical ball-bearing rattling and spinning slot reels.
  - `crb:hakari.riichi_fanfare`: Tense fanfare building up during final roll confirmation.
  - `crb:hakari.jackpot_ding`: Euphoric multi-tone bell signaling 7-7-7 alignment!
