- **JJA Internal Namespace / Folders**: `functions/lovetrain/`, `items/lovetrain/`, tags `hakarimode`, `domainlayout`

## 2. Canon Lore Summary
Kinji Hakari is an extraordinary sorcerer recognized by Gojo as someone capable of standing shoulder-to-shoulder with him. Hakari's cursed energy possesses an innate rough, abrasive edge that makes even ordinary blows feel like being struck by a baseball bat wrapped in sandpaper. His innate technique revolves around the romance manga *Private Pure Love Train*. Like Higuruma, Hakari's technique is fundamentally centered on his domain expansion, Idle Death Gamble. Inside, a pachinko machine game unfolds based on visual effect indicators, chance meetings, and slot spins. Hitting the Jackpot grants Hakari boundless, overflowing cursed energy for the duration of the manga's theme song (4 minutes and 11 seconds), causing his body to perform reflex, automatic Reverse Cursed Technique that renders him completely unkillable.
