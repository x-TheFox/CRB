# Character Dossier: Ryomen Sukuna (両面 宿儺)

## 1. Identity & Technique Profile
- **Character**: Ryomen Sukuna — The King of Curses, The Disgraced One
- **Innate Technique**: **Shrine (御厨子, Mizushi)**
- **Domain Expansion**: **Malevolent Shrine (伏魔御厨子, Fukuma Mizushi)**
- **Special Forms**: Heian Era Four-Armed True Form (`ds:heian_transformation`), Meguna (Megumi Vessel Takeover, `meguna_cutscene.mcfunction`)
- **JJA Internal Namespace / Folders**: `functions/sukuna/`, `items/sukuna/`, tags `has_sukuna`, `sukuna`, `heian`

## 2. Canon Lore Summary
Ryomen Sukuna is the strongest sorcerer in history, reigning unchallenged during the golden age of jujutsu in the Heian era. Feared as a calamity, his innate technique "Shrine" allows him to slice and dissect targets through two distinct slash variants: "Dismantle" (ordinary invisible slashes for inanimate objects or non-CE targets) and "Cleave" (slashes that adjust to the target's toughness and cursed energy density to cut them down in one fell swoop). Sukuna can also manipulate thermochemical cursed energy through "Open: Divine Flame" (Fuga), launching devastating fire arrows. In his true form, Sukuna possesses four arms and two mouths, allowing continuous chanting and hand-sign formation without interrupting combat mobility.

## 3. Progression & Unlock Arc in JJA
1. **Vessel Ingestion Arc**: Finding and eating `ds:sukuna_finger` initiates a survival check (`functions/relics/sukuna_finger.mcfunction`). Consuming 1 finger unlocks basic Dismantle. Consuming 5, 10, 15, and 20 fingers unlocks Cleave, Malevolent Shrine, Fuga: Divine Flame, and World Cutting Slash.
2. **Technique Item**: `ds:sukuna_technique` provides access to the complete suite.
3. **Fighting Styles**:
   - `ds:sukuna_fists`: Ryomen Sukuna's Fighting Style
   - `ds:cleave_fists`: Slashing Fighting Style
   - `ds:four_fists`: Four-Handed Fighting Style (unlocked via Heian Transformation)
4. **Transformations**:
   - `ds:heian_transformation`: Reincarnates into 4-armed Heian form with four active fist hitboxes and double chanting speed.
   - `meguna_cutscene`: Triggers takeover of Megumi Fushiguro's body, gaining access to both Shrine and Ten Shadows (Mahoraga).
