# Ryomen Sukuna — JJA Parity Checklist

This checklist defines the complete, itemized parity contract between the JJA Bedrock addon and the CRB Java Edition mod for Ryomen Sukuna. Every item must be verified green before Phase 4 completion for this character.

## 1. Core Mechanics & Forms
- [ ] **Vessel Ingestion & Awakening**: Consuming Sukuna's Fingers unlocks abilities progressively.
- [ ] **Heian Era Transformation**: Reincarnation into 4-armed form granting extra limb combat hitboxes and rapid chanting.
- [ ] **Meguna Form**: Takeover of Megumi Fushiguro enabling dual-access to Shrine and Ten Shadows.
- [ ] **Water Running**: Can sprint across water without sinking (`waterrun2.mcfunction`).

## 2. Active Abilities & Slashes
- [ ] **Basic Dismantle**: Forward invisible slicing projectile (`ds:dismantle`).
- [ ] **Quick Dismantle**: Rapid flick slash with increased velocity (`ds:dismantle_quick`).
- [ ] **Dismantle Barrage / Pummel**: Fan of slicing blades and rapid melee slice flurry.
- [ ] **Contact Cleave**: Grabs target and deals toughness-scaling lethal slice damage.
- [ ] **Cleave: Spiderweb**: Smashes palm into ground, tracing a geometric web of slashes across terrain.
- [ ] **Cleave Counter**: Cinematic melee counter parry slicing opponent point-blank.
- [ ] **Open: Divine Flame (Fuga)**: Flaming bow and arrow dealing 150 fire damage and igniting 20-block crater.
- [ ] **World Cutting Slash**: Space-cleaving slash requiring vocal chants and hand sign, dealing 999 true damage and bisecting targets.

## 3. Fighting Styles
- [ ] **Sukuna's Fighting Style** (`ds:sukuna_fists`): Demonic Blows, Hellish Descent, Open Space, Step, Takedown, Demonic Blitz.
- [ ] **Slashing Fighting Style** (`ds:cleave_fists`): Carve, Rushing Cleave, Dual Cleave, Rapid Cleaves, Cleave Combo.
- [ ] **Four-Handed Fighting Style** (`ds:four_fists`): Double Impact, Demonic Leap, Obliterate, Overwhelming Power.

## 4. Domain Expansion: Malevolent Shrine
- [ ] **Open-Barrier Structure**: No outer wall; full 200-block operational radius.
- [ ] **Center Monument**: Demonic shrine structure spawns at center.
- [ ] **Continuous Sure-Hit**: Relentlessly strikes entities with Cleave and terrain with Dismantle.
- [ ] **Thermobaric Fuga Synergy**: Casting Fuga inside/after Malevolent Shrine triggers a catastrophic dust explosion.

## 5. Audio, VFX & Presentation
- [ ] All voice lines (*"Kai"*, *"Hachi"*, *"Fuga"*, chants) accurately wired.
- [ ] World Cutting Slash monochrome impact frame and diagonal screen displacement.
- [ ] 200m red skybox atmospheric tint during Malevolent Shrine.
