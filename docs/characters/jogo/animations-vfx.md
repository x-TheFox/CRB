# Jogo — Animations, VFX & Presentation Specification

## 1. Skeletal Animations (`animations/`)
From `JJA RP AND BP/Resource/JujutsuAwa/animations/`:
- `animation.jogo.head_erupt`: Head volcano geyser releasing pressurized steam and molten lava during enrage or ability casts.
- `animation.jogo.fire_palm`: Thrusting open clawed palms forward with fire discharge recoil.
- `animation.jogo.meteor_lift`: Raising both arms high overhead with back arched as the colossal meteor manifests.
- `animation.jogo.handsign_coffin`: Clasping hands together forming a volcanic caldera funnel sign with index and thumbs.

## 2. Particle Pipeline & VFX Assets
- `particles/flames/magma_stream.json`: Liquid orange-yellow fluid lava stream with floating embers and smoke wisps.
- `particles/flames/ember_insect.json`: Glowing red insect wing trail with high-pitch sparking halo.
- `particles/flames/meteor_crater.json`: Expanding radial ring of volcanic fire, shockwave dust, and flying lava droplets.
- `particles/flames/iron_mountain_heat.json`: Heavy orange-red atmospheric heat haze distorting all geometry within the domain.

## 3. Attachables & Models
- `models/entity/jogo.geo.json`: One-eyed volcanic disaster curse model with head caldera and cape.
- `models/entity/meteor.geo.json`: Massive 20-block diameter burning asteroid model with glowing magma veins.
