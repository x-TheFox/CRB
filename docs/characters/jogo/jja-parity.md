# Jogo — JJA Parity Checklist

This checklist defines the complete, itemized parity contract between the JJA Bedrock addon and the CRB Java Edition mod for Jogo. Every item must be verified green before Phase 4 completion for this character.

## 1. Core Mechanics & Passives
- [ ] **Curse Physiology**: Natural fire immunity, night vision, and regeneration through negative cursed energy.
- [ ] **Head Volcano Eruption**: Steaming head geyser on combat entry and taking damage.

## 2. Active Abilities
- [ ] **Flamethrower (`ds:flames1`)**: Sustained short-range fire breath cone.
- [ ] **Fire Palm**: Burst of flame from palms setting enemies ablaze.
- [ ] **Ember Insects (`ds:flames2`)**: 4 flying homing insects with screech audio and explosive impact.
- [ ] **Fireball (`ds:flames3`)**: Ballistic molten projectile.
- [ ] **Flame Choke**: Sneak melee grab incinerating target from within.
- [ ] **Volcanic Launch (`ds:flames5`)**: Ground eruption launching targets skyward.
- [ ] **Maximum Meteor (`ds:flames6`)**: Giant descending asteroid leveling a 35-block crater.

- [ ] **Passive Heat Burn**: Entities without CE or Simple Domain take continuous fire damage.
- [ ] **Sure-Hit Magma Spikes**: Volcanic stone spikes erupting directly into targets.
