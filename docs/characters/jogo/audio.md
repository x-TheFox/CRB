- `flames.ember_buzz`: High-pitched screeching insect buzz (`sounds/flames/insect_buzz.ogg`).

- `flames.meteor_impact`: Catastrophic ground impact explosion (`sounds/flames/meteor_hit.ogg`).
- `flames.domain_ambient`: Continuous bubbling lava cauldron and cracking rock interior.
