# Character Dossier: Jogo (漏瑚)

## 1. Identity & Technique Profile
- **Character**: Jogo — Special Grade Cursed Spirit (Disaster Curse of the Earth & Fire)
- **Innate Technique**: **Disaster Flames (災禍の炎, Saika no Honō)**
- **Domain Expansion**: **Coffin of the Iron Mountain (蓋棺鉄囲山, Gaikan Tecchisen)**
- **Maximum Technique**: **Maximum: Meteor (極ノ番「隕石」, Gokunoban: Inseki)**
- **JJA Internal Namespace / Folders**: `functions/flames/`, `items/disaster_flames/`, tags `flames`, `curse`, `jogo`

## 2. Canon Lore Summary
Jogo is an unregistered Special Grade Cursed Spirit born from humanity's collective negative emotions toward the earth and volcanic disasters. Proud of being a "true human" born of pure malice without falsehoods, Jogo wields immense destructive cursed energy, capable of spontaneously incinerating crowds of sorcerers in a split second. His technique allows him to generate intense thermal flames, manifest flying volcanic insects that chirp before detonating, erupt lava geysers from terrain, and summon a colossal flaming meteor capable of leveling an entire city district. His domain, Coffin of the Iron Mountain, traps victims inside the molten heart of an active volcano where the ambient thermal heat alone incinerates average sorcerers.
