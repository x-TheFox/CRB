# Hajime Kashimo — Audio & Sound Design

## 1. Sound Assets & Events
- **Bedrock Sound Events**:
  - `mob.kashimoscream`: Bloodcurdling battle yell during cloak activation and Amber burst.
  - `ambient.weather.thunder`: Pitched variations (0.5x, 0.7x, 1.0x, 1.25x, 1.75x) representing sonic crack of near-distance discharge.
  - `mob.lightning.spark`: Crackling static electricity loop on melee hits.
  - `ds.kashimo.amber_hum`: Low-frequency humming oscillation while in Mythical Beast Amber state.
- **Java SoundEvent Registry Entries**:
  - `crb:kashimo.electrified_hit`: Heavy physical thud accompanied by high-voltage snap.
  - `crb:kashimo.sure_hit_discharge`: Immediate sonic detonation without pre-strike warning.
  - `crb:kashimo.cloak_activate`: Roaring turbine electrical spinup.
  - `crb:kashimo.amber_awaken`: Godly atmospheric rumble that shakes audio mix.
  - `crb:kashimo.amber_voice`: Distorted dual-channel voice lines.
