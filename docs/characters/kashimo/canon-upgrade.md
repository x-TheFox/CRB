# Hajime Kashimo — Canon Upgrades (§5.4)

## 1. Canon Abilities Missing or Truncated in JJA
1. **Electrolysis Water Detonation (電気分解)**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 189 (Kashimo vs Hakari). Kashimo falls into the ocean with Hakari and channels his immense electrical output to electrolyze the seawater, separating water into hydrogen and oxygen gas to trigger a massive thermobaric explosion that blows off Hakari's arm.
   - *JJA omission*: JJA had a rudimentary `water_conduct.mcfunction` that dealt flat damage in water without hydrogen accumulation or explosion.
   - *CRB Elevation*: When Kashimo discharges electricity in a body of water (minimum 8 water blocks), bubbles of gas generate for 3 seconds before detonating in a colossal fire/explosion with power 6.0, consuming all oxygen and inflicting drowning damage on submerged survivors.

2. **Chlorine Gas Poisoning (塩素ガス発生)**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 189. Kashimo's electrolysis of salt water generates toxic chlorine gas, poisoning Hakari and forcing him to instinctively execute reverse cursed technique through his nose.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Submerged or water-adjacent discharge in ocean/swamp biomes releases a green chlorine gas cloud (`crb:chlorine_gas`) inflicting severe Nausea, Blindness, and Poison IV unless the target has advanced RCT.

3. **Hollow Wicker Basket (彌虚葛籠, Yayeko Tsuzura)**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 187. Kashimo creates a woven wicker barrier of CE using hand signs to neutralize an enemy's domain sure-hit.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Native implementation of Hollow Wicker Basket as Kashimo's primary Anti-Domain Art, requiring both hands to maintain posture.

4. **Irreversible Corporeal Vaporization**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 238. Mythical Beast Amber causes the flesh to vaporize into electricity, irrevocably destroying Kashimo once the technique completes.
   - *CRB Elevation*: High-stakes cinematic disintegration animation on death with ash remnants and cursed tool drops.
