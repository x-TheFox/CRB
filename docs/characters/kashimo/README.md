## 2. Canon Lore Summary
Hajime Kashimo is a reincarnated ancient sorcerer from 400 years ago who stood undefeated at the peak of the Edo period. Kashimo's cursed energy possesses an innate electrical property: simply channeling it into physical strikes causes a continuous stinging electric discharge that bypasses ordinary guard. Through repeated melee hits, Kashimo separates positive and negative electrical charges between himself and his opponent. Once charged, Kashimo can discharge a lethal bolt of lightning directly through the target without needing a domain expansion—a guaranteed-hit lightning strike! Kashimo's single-use innate technique, Mythical Beast Amber, reconstructs his physical flesh into any electrical phenomenon, granting godlike speed, sound-wave cannons, and electromagnetic vaporization rays, at the cost of evaporating his body once deactivated.
