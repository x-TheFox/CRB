## 2. Concept & Lore Summary
Absorption is a unique cursed technique featured in Jujutsu Awakening centered on dynamic energy transference. When struck by physical impacts, elemental cursed techniques, or environmental forces, the user's cursed energy acts as a high-density sponge that captures and compresses the kinetic and spiritual energy of the attack into an internal reservoir. This stored energy does not decay immediately; instead, the user can vent it outward in escalating retaliatory discharges: short-range electrified counter-punches, piercing plasma beams, seismic shockwaves, and at maximum charge, a devastating tactical blast that rivals a miniature nuclear detonation.
