# Kinetic & Curse Absorption — Domain Status & Anti-Domain Arts

## 1. Domain Status
- **Innate Domain Expansion**: Absorption is a non-canon JJA original technique and possesses **no innate domain expansion** in JJA.
- **Anti-Domain Defense: Falling Blossom Emotion (落花の情, Rakka no Jō)**:
  - Because Absorption specializes in kinetic and energy redirection, its primary anti-domain art is Falling Blossom Emotion.
  - When caught inside a foreign domain expansion, Falling Blossom Emotion automatically intercepts incoming sure-hit attacks with equal opposing bursts of stored absorption energy, nullifying the attack at the cost of consuming stored charge.
