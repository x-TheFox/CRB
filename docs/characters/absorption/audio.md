# Kinetic & Curse Absorption — Audio & Sound Design

## 1. Sound Assets & Events
- **Bedrock references**:
  - `music.nuke`: Apocalyptic siren and deep choral hum during the ultimate charge.
  - `mob.electricity`: Crackling static electricity loop.
  - `mob.shock1`: Sharp electric impact.
- **Java SoundEvent Registry Entries**:
  - `crb:absorption.guard_hit`: Metallic ping followed by a deep bass absorption swallow.
  - `crb:absorption.beam_hum`: Resonant continuous laser turbine sound.
  - `crb:absorption.nuke_detonate`: Colossal cinematic explosion with long low-frequency subsonic tail.
