# Kinetic & Curse Absorption — System Elevations (§5.4)

## 1. Upgrades to JJA Original Design
Because Absorption is a JJA-original technique rather than a canon manga ability, elevations focus on transforming Bedrock command hacks into a deeply satisfying, competitive combat system:

1. **Parry Window & Perfect Absorption (ジャストガード吸収)**:
   - *CRB Elevation*: Timing an absorption guard within 4 ticks of an incoming melee attack or projectile absorbs **100% of the damage**, generates +25 bonus charge, and immediately breaks the attacker's guard.

2. **Elemental Energy Conversion**:
   - *CRB Elevation*: Storing damage from specific elements grants temporary infusion properties:
     - Absorbing fire/magma (Jogo) converts discharges into incendiary blast waves.
     - Absorbing electricity (Kashimo) adds chain-lightning stun arcs to subsequent punches.
     - Absorbing spatial attacks (Gojo/Sukuna) charges the ultimate supernova 2x faster.

3. **Dynamic Charge Overload**:
   - *CRB Elevation*: If the player absorbs more than 100 damage without venting it within 10 seconds, the reserve enters an "Overload" state—granting massive speed and strength buffs while venting sparks that damage adjacent enemies, but ticking small self-bleed if not discharged.
