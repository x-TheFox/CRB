# Character Dossier: Yuji Itadori (虎杖 悠仁)

## 1. Identity & Technique Profile
- **Character**: Yuji Itadori — The Tiger of West Junior High, The Prince of Black Sparks
- **Innate Trait / Physicality**: Superhuman Physical Prowess (Blessed Strength / Kenjaku's vessel design)
- **Innate Techniques**:
  - **Soul Shrine (魂の御厨子, Tamashii no Mizushi)**: Soul Dismantle & Cleave
  - **Blood Manipulation (赤血操術, Sekketsu Sōjutsu)**: Acquired via Death Painting Wombs ingestion
- **Core Combat Arts**: Divergent Fist (逕庭拳, Keiteiken), Consecutive Black Flash (黒閃, Kokusen), "The Zone"
- **Domain Expansion**: **Shrine of Reverie (虎杖悠仁の領域展開)**: Yuji's nostalgic train platform & town domain
- **JJA Internal Namespace / Folders**: `functions/yuji/`, `items/yuji/`, tags `yuji`, `divergentfists`, `blackflash`

## 2. Canon Lore Summary
Yuji Itadori is the protagonist of Jujutsu Kaisen, created by Kenjaku with innate superhuman physical strength and an unbreakable body capable of withstanding Ryomen Sukuna's soul. As he develops, Yuji masters Cursed Energy application through Divergent Fist (a double-impact blow where cursed energy lags behind the physical strike). Yuji becomes the "beloved child of the black sparks", holding the all-time record for consecutive Black Flash strikes (8 consecutive in Shinjuku). By awakening his soul awareness through Yuki Tsukumo's research, Yuji develops Soul Dismantles that strike directly at the boundary between souls, tearing Sukuna out of Megumi Fushiguro. In the climax of Shinjuku, Yuji manifests his own Domain Expansion.
