# Yuji Itadori — JJA Parity Checklist

This checklist defines the complete, itemized parity contract between the JJA Bedrock addon and the CRB Java Edition mod for Yuji Itadori. Every item must be verified green before Phase 4 completion for this character.

## 1. Core Mechanics & Passives
- [ ] **Superhuman Prowess**: Innate +12 Strength and +12 Agility scaling without weapon dependency.
- [ ] **Divergent Fist**: Two-phase punch impact with delayed secondary cursed energy shockwave.
- [ ] **Black Flash Chain**: Can chain consecutive Black Flashes up to 8 times with increasing damage multipliers.
- [ ] **"The Zone" State**: Enters The Zone upon Black Flash hit, buffing speed and reducing CE costs.
- [ ] **Water Running**: Can sprint across water surfaces at high speed.

## 2. Active Abilities
- [ ] **Brutal Quake**: Ground-slam punch shattering earth and launching nearby targets.
- [ ] **Deadly Pursuit**: High-speed sprint tackle closing gaps instantly.
- [ ] **Fierce Strikes**: Rapid 5-hit boxing combination ending with an uppercut.
- [ ] **Twin Kicks**: Mid-air dropkick combo (`twinkicks.mcfunction`).
- [ ] **Vengeance Fists**: High-damage revenge counter following taking damage.
- [ ] **Rock Throw**: Hurling ground debris at distant enemies (`rock_throw.mcfunction`).
- [ ] **Soul Dismantle**: Direct scissor-shaped slash targeting the boundary between souls.

## 3. Domain Expansion: Shrine of Reverie
- [ ] **Station Arena Generation**: Procedurally manifests the nostalgic train station platform environment.
- [ ] **Soul Strike Sure-Hit**: All melee hits target the soul directly and bypass armor.
- [ ] **Guaranteed Black Flash**: 4th consecutive hit inside domain triggers guaranteed Black Flash.

## 4. Audio, VFX & Presentation
- [ ] Divergent Fist dual-impact sound and particle delay verified.
- [ ] Black Flash spatial distortion lightning and monochrome impact frames verified.
- [ ] Japanese voice lines wired.
