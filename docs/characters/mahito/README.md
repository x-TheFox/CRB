# Character Dossier: Mahito (真人)

- **Innate Technique**: **Idle Transfiguration (無為転変, Mui Tenpen)**
- **Domain Expansion**: **Self-Embodiment of Perfection (自閉円頓裹, Jihei Endonka)**
- **Ultimate Form**: **Instant Spirit Body of Distorted Killing (遍殺即霊体, Hensatsu Sokureitai)**
- **JJA Internal Namespace / Folders**: `functions/idle_transfig/`, `items/c_idle_transfig/`, tags `idlect`, `curse`, `mahito`

## 2. Canon Lore Summary
Mahito is an unregistered Special Grade Cursed Spirit born from the hatred, fear, and malice that humans harbor toward other humans. Childlike, playful, and completely devoid of empathy, Mahito views humans as toys and experimentation material. His innate technique, Idle Transfiguration, grants him the ability to touch and reshape the contours of the soul. Because the physical body conforms to the shape of the soul, transfiguring the soul instantaneously mutates the body into grotesque forms or kills the target. Mahito is virtually immune to conventional physical attacks because he maintains the shape of his own soul, effortlessly reforming from bullets, slashes, and crushing blows. Only sorcerers aware of the soul (Yuji Itadori) or weapons that pierce the soul (Split-Soul Katana, Nobara Kugisaki's Resonance) can harm him.
