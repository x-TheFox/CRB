# Mahito — Canon Completion & Upgrade Specification

## 1. Missing-Iconic Analysis (Canon Gap Identification)

### 1.1 Complete Polymorphic Soul Isomer (多重魂・幾魂異性体)
- **Canon Ref**: Chapters 129–130 (Shibuya Incident vs. Yuji & Todo)
- **Gap in JJA**: JJA provides individual transfigured humans (`e_transfigured_human1` and `2`), but lacks the combined **Polymorphic Soul Isomer** (fusing dozens of rejected souls to create an overwhelmingly strong, fast behemoth capable of challenging Grade 1 sorcerers).
- **Java Upgrade Spec**:
  - Ability: **Polymorphic Soul Isomer**
  - Trigger: Consume 5 stored soul vials while in Soul Stance.
  - Effect: Summons a hulking, elongated soul construct with 150 HP and high attack damage (45 damage per swing) that lasts 20 seconds.

### 1.2 Body Splitting / Doppelgänger Decoy
- **Canon Ref**: Chapters 121–125 (Splitting into two bodies to fight Yuji and Nobara simultaneously)
- **Gap in JJA**: In JJA, Mahito is always a single entity. Canonically, he can split his body into two autonomous entities (one retaining Idle Transfiguration, the other acting as an agile decoy with physical weapons).
- **Java Upgrade Spec**:
  - Ability: **Soul Division: Decoy Split**
  - Effect: Splits health pool 50/50, spawning an autonomous decoy Mahito that fights with blade arms while the main body flanks.

## 2. Fidelity Corrections
1. **Soul Awareness Damage Rule**: In JJA, regular swords deal full damage to Mahito. In canon (Chapter 21), physical attacks without soul perception deal 0 damage. In CRB, players without soul awareness (those who haven't coexisted with a curse or trained in soul perception) deal negligible chip damage that Mahito immediately regenerates!

## 3. Flagged Judgment Calls (Orchestrator Adjudication)
- **Judgment Call: Instant-Kill on Players via Touch**:
  - In canon, Mahito can kill low-grade humans in 1 touch, but sorcerers with strong CE protect their souls.
  - *Ruling*: **Non-Sorcerers / Mobs = 1 Touch Kill; Players = True Scaling Soul Damage (35–50 HP per touch)**. This maintains canon lethality without creating an unplayable one-shot mechanic against geared sorcerers.
