# Toge Inumaki — Canon Upgrades (§5.4)

## 1. Canon Abilities Missing or Truncated in JJA
1. **Shinjuku Audio Recording Tape Broadcast**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 262 (Yuta & Inumaki plan vs Sukuna). Inumaki records his voice onto a cursed tape recorder carried by a crow / shikigami, detonating the command *"Don't Move!"* directly into Sukuna's ear from point-blank range while Inumaki is miles away.
   - *JJA omission*: Completely absent from JJA.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Craftable **Cursed Voice Recorder (呪言の録音機)**. Inumaki can record a single command into the cassette; placing or tossing the recorder detonates the command upon remote trigger or player proximity.

2. **Cursed Energy Ear Defense Interaction (耳の呪力防御)**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 44 (Kyoto Goodwill Event - Kamo vs Inumaki). Sorcerers who know Cursed Speech is coming can protect their brains and eardrums by reinforcing their ears with cursed energy, mitigating the severity of the command.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Active guard mechanic: If an enemy blocks or channels CE into defense when hearing a command, stun duration and damage are reduced by 60%.

3. **Complex Vocabulary Rice Ball Communication**:
   - *Canon ref*: Canon vocabulary: Takana (concern/disapproval), Shake (agreement/yes), Okaka (disagreement/no), Tsuna Maya (neutral statement).
   - *CRB Elevation*: In-game chat filter: When playing as Inumaki with the collar zipped, all public text chat is automatically translated into authentic rice-ball vocabulary with subtitle tooltips for allies.
