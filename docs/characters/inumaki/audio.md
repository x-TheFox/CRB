# Toge Inumaki — Audio & Sound Design

## 1. Sound Assets & Events
- **Bedrock references**:
  - Voice lines for commands (`dont_move`, `sleep`, `blast_away`, `plummet`, `die`).
  - Coughing sound effects.
  - Acoustic distortion echo.
- **Java SoundEvent Registry Entries**:
  - `crb:inumaki.unzip`: Crisp metallic zipper sound.
  - `crb:inumaki.voice_ugokuna`: Resonant layered voice line: *"Ugokuna!"*
  - `crb:inumaki.voice_nemure`: Soft whispered echo: *"Nemure..."*
  - `crb:inumaki.voice_buttobe`: Explosive shouting snap: *"Buttobe!"*
  - `crb:inumaki.voice_ochiro`: Deep heavy command: *"Ochiro!"*
  - `crb:inumaki.voice_shine`: Blood-curdling supreme command: *"Shine!"*
  - `crb:inumaki.throat_choke`: Painful dry gasping and coughing blood.
  - `crb:inumaki.medicine_spray`: Pressurized aerosol hiss.
