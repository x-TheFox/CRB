## 2. Canon Lore Summary
Toge Inumaki is a descendant of the Inumaki Clan, born with the Snake and Fangs crest etched onto his tongue and the corners of his mouth. His technique, Cursed Speech, imbues his spoken words with cursed energy, compelling anyone who hears them to obey the command unconditionally. The severity of the command scales with the difference in strength between Inumaki and his target: simple commands like "Stop" or "Sleep" exert minimal strain, while destructive commands like "Plummet", "Blast Away", or "Die" inflict immense rebound damage on Inumaki's throat, causing severe coughing, hemorrhaging, and vocal cord tearing against powerful curses or sorcerers. To prevent accidental activation, Inumaki communicates exclusively in rice ball fillings (Salmon, Bonito Flakes, Kelp).
