# Toji Fushiguro & Maki Zenin — Canon Upgrades (§5.4)

## 1. Canon Abilities Missing or Truncated in JJA
1. **Air-Stepping / Atmosphere Footholds (大気への跳躍)**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 197 (Maki vs Curse Naoya). Maki realizes the air is not empty space but a tangible medium composed of varying densities, allowing her to kick off air molecules to change direction mid-flight freely.
   - *JJA omission*: JJA had standard Minecraft jump boost without mid-air vector re-direction.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Multi-jump aerial maneuverability: can jump up to 3 times while airborne by kicking the atmosphere.

2. **Perception of Inorganic Surfaces & Soul Boundaries (無生物の魂の視認)**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 198. Toji and Maki perceive the outline of souls within all things—living entities and inorganic matter alike.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Dedicated sensory toggle allowing players to see block densities, underground cavities, and soul health bars through walls within 24 blocks.

3. **Chain of a Thousand Miles & Inverted Spear Whiplash (万里ノ鎖の無限伸長)**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 73. Toji attaches the Inverted Spear of Heaven to the Chain of a Thousand Miles, swinging it at blinding speeds across hundreds of meters while keeping the anchor concealed in the worm curse's mouth.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Ranged grappling flail mechanic allowing the Inverted Spear to be whipped outward up to 30 blocks to hook enemies or terrain.
