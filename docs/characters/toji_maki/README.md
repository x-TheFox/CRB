## 2. Canon Lore Summary
Toji Fushiguro and post-awakening Maki Zenin possess a Heavenly Restriction that completely eradicated all cursed energy from their bodies—a true "Zero CE" state that makes them invisible to sorcerer senses and domain barriers. In exchange, the world bestowed upon them bodies of transcendent physical prowess: reflexes that outrun lightning, 5 heightened senses capable of seeing air density, temperature differentials, and surfaces, an immune system impervious to curses, and sheer muscular power that demolishes Special Grade spirits with raw brute force. Because domain barriers treat them as inanimate objects (like buildings or stones), domains cannot trap them without their explicit consent, and automated sure-hit effects cannot target them.
