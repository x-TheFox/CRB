# Toji Fushiguro & Maki Zenin — Summons & Entities Specification

## 1. Entities & Cursed Tools
- **Inventory Curse (格納呪霊, Worm Curse)**:
  - Slender worm curse coiled around Toji's torso.
  - Acts as an extra-dimensional portable weapon storage (holding Inverted Spear, Soul Sword, Playful Cloud, Flyheads, and Thousand Mile Chain).
  - Shrinks and hides inside Toji's stomach when stealth is needed.
- **Swarm Flyheads Entity (`crb:flyhead_swarm`)**:
  - Cloud of low-grade flyhead cursed spirits released to obscure vision and overwhelm sorcerer cursed energy sensors.
- **Chain of a Thousand Miles Entity (`crb:chain_thousand_miles`)**:
  - Procedural physics chain attached to Inverted Spear, extending infinitely as long as the opposite end is concealed in the inventory curse.
- **Boss Entities**:
  - `crb:toji_fushiguro_boss`: High-difficulty world boss featuring multi-weapon switching and dynamic tactical repositioning.
  - `crb:maki_zenin_boss`: Awakened Zenin Clan slaughterer boss wielding the Split Soul Katana.
