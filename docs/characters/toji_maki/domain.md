# Toji Fushiguro & Maki Zenin — Domain Interaction & Natural Immunity

## 1. Domain Status
- **Innate Domain Expansion**: Heavenly Restriction users possess **no innate domain** and **no anti-domain techniques** (Simple Domain, DA, HWB).
- **The Ultimate Canon Rule: The Phantom Entity (透明人間)**:
  - *Canon ref*: Chapters 197–198 (Maki vs Cursed Naoya's Time Cell Moon Palace).
  - *Server-Authoritative Enforcement*:
    1. **Barrier Infiltration**: Standard enclosed domain barriers treat zero-CE individuals as inanimate physical objects (like rocks or buildings). They can enter and leave a domain barrier freely at any time without having to break the barrier from the outside.
    2. **Sure-Hit Invalidation**: Domain sure-hit attacks rely on locking onto the cursed energy signature of entities within the barrier. Because Toji and Maki have exactly zero cursed energy, the sure-hit cannot register or target them.
    3. **Open-Barrier Domain Exception**: In open-barrier domains with an environmental physical sure-hit (like Sukuna's Malevolent Shrine using Cleave on objects with CE and Dismantle on inanimate objects), Dismantle **will** slice zero-CE individuals since it targets non-cursed matter.
