# Toji Fushiguro & Maki Zenin — Audio & Sound Design

## 1. Sound Assets & Events
- **Bedrock references**:
  - `mob.toji.slash`: Heavy steel blade slice.
  - Sonic boom crack on jetstream dash.
  - Crushing bone fracture audio on bone splitter.
- **Java SoundEvent Registry Entries**:
  - `crb:toji.air_step`: Compressed sonic pop when stepping off air.
  - `crb:toji.spear_nullify`: High-pitch glass-shattering cancellation chime when Inverted Spear dispels a technique.
  - `crb:toji.soul_slice`: Eerie harmonic resonance echoing from a direct soul wound.
  - `crb:toji.inventory_worm`: Wet guttural regurgitation audio when drawing weapons from the inventory curse.
