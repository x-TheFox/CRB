# Hiromi Higuruma — Canon Upgrades (§5.4)

## 1. Canon Abilities Missing or Truncated in JJA
1. **Domain Amplification (領域展延, Ryōiki Ten'en)**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 246 (vs Sukuna). Higuruma masters Domain Amplification on the fly, fluidly turning it on and off between attacks to bypass Sukuna's Dismantle while retaining his technique.
   - *JJA omission*: Higuruma was given no Domain Amplification capability in JJA.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Full Domain Amplification toggle allowing Higuruma to neutralize incoming cursed technique attacks at the cost of temporarily setting his Gavel to standard blunt damage.

2. **Reverse Cursed Technique Awakening**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 247. In his final clash with Sukuna, Higuruma awakens RCT on the verge of death to regenerate his severed limb.
   - *CRB Elevation*: When dropping below 15% HP for the first time in a fight, Higuruma has an instinctual trigger chance to unlock RCT healing.

3. **Cursed Tool Confiscation Priority (Kamutoke Loophole)**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 245. When Sukuna is sentenced to Confiscation, Judgeman confiscates the cursed dagger Kamutoke instead of his Shrine technique due to judicial precedent.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Strict adherence to canon order of confiscation:
     - Priority 1: Cursed Tool in inventory or hand is locked/confiscated.
     - Priority 2: If no cursed tool, Innate Technique is sealed.
     - Priority 3: If no innate technique (e.g. Yuji/HR), raw Cursed Energy manipulation is suppressed.

4. **Passing the Executioner's Sword to Allies**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 247. As Higuruma falls, he hurls the glowing Executioner's Sword to Yuji Itadori, and the sword remains lethal to Sukuna because Higuruma's curse strengthens after death.
   - *CRB Elevation*: If Higuruma drops or gives the Executioner's Sword to an allied player, that player can deliver the fatal blow to the condemned target before the blade disintegrates.
