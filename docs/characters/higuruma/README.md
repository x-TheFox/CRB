# Character Dossier: Hiromi Higuruma (日車 寛見)

## 1. Identity & Technique Profile
- **Character**: Hiromi Higuruma — Defense Attorney turned Culling Game Sorcerer Prodigy
- **Cursed Technique**: **Deadly Sentencing (死刑宣告)** & **Judgeman (ジャッジマン)**
- **Innate Domain / Domain Expansion**: **Deadly Sentencing (誅伏賜死, Chūbuku Shishi)**
- **Iconic Weapons**: Judge's Gavel (変形する小槌) & Executioner's Sword (処刑人の剣)
- **JJA Internal Namespace / Folders**: `functions/judgeman/`, `items/judgeman/`, tags `the_judge`, `on_trial`, `confiscated`

## 2. Canon Lore Summary
Hiromi Higuruma awakened his cursed technique during the Culling Game. Because modern sorcery law evolved backwards from old-style non-lethal domains, Higuruma's domain expansion is the default fundamental application of his technique rather than an ultimate summit. His domain constitutes a modern courtroom overseen by his shikigami, Judgeman. Inside, physical violence is strictly banned by a binding vow. A trial ensues where the defendant is prosecuted for past crimes based on evidence sealed in Judgeman's envelope. Depending on the verdict, Judgeman imposes sentences: **Confiscation** (nullifies cursed technique, or confiscated cursed tool) and **Death Penalty** (grants Higuruma the Executioner's Sword, an instant-kill blade that extinguishes the target's life upon the lightest scratch).
