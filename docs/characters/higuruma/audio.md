# Hiromi Higuruma — Audio & Sound Design

## 1. Sound Assets & Events
- **Bedrock references**:
  - `ambient.soulsand_valley.mood`: Low eerie drone inside Deadly Sentencing.
  - `block.wood.hit` / `random.anvil_land`: Gavel hammer strikes.
  - `mob.judgeman.gavel`: Deep echoing gavel pound (Order in the court!).
  - `mob.judgeman.guilty`: Pronouncement chime and gavel smash.
- **Java SoundEvent Registry Entries**:
  - `crb:higuruma.gavel_hit`: Crisp wooden impact resonating with cursed energy.
  - `crb:higuruma.gavel_grow`: Metallic ratcheting sound of telescoping extension.
  - `crb:judgeman.court_bell`: Resonant brass courtroom bell ringing at trial start.
  - `crb:judgeman.verdict_guilty`: Thunderous triple gavel strike echoing off wood-paneled walls.
  - `crb:judgeman.verdict_innocent`: Melodic soft chime of dismissal.
  - `crb:executionsword.hum`: Ethereal divine choral chord on sword draw.
