# Character Dossier: Satoru Gojo (五条 悟)

## 1. Identity & Technique Profile
- **Character**: Satoru Gojo — "The Strongest", Head of the Gojo Clan
- **Innate Technique**: **Limitless (無下限呪術, Mukagen Jujutsu)**
- **Innate Trait / Eye Phenomenon**: **The Six Eyes (六眼, Rikugan)**
- **Domain Expansion**: **Unlimited Void (無量空処, Muryōkūsho)**
- **JJA Internal Namespace / Folders**: `functions/limitless/`, `items/limitless/`, tags `limitless`, `gojo`, `sixeyes`, `honored`

## 2. Canon Lore Summary
Satoru Gojo is the contemporary pinnacle of jujutsu sorcery, born with the once-in-four-hundred-years confluence of both the Limitless cursed technique and the Six Eyes. Limitless grants precise manipulation of space at an atomic scale, bringing the mathematical concept of infinity into physical reality. Neutral Limitless creates an absolute spatial barrier that decelerates incoming attacks exponentially so they never reach him. Reinforced by Cursed Technique Lapse: Blue (attractive force), Cursed Technique Reversal: Red (repulsive force), and Secret Art: Hollow Purple (imaginary mass annihilation), Gojo dominates the jujutsu world. His Six Eyes allow atomic cursed energy perception and reduce cursed energy consumption to near-absolute zero.

## 3. Progression & Unlock Arc in JJA
1. **Clan & Trait Rolling**: Gojo Clan (3% chance) grants eligibility for `The Six Eyes` trait (`functions/menu_traitshow.mcfunction`). Six Eyes drastically cuts CE costs and enhances CE perception.
2. **Technique Acquisition**: Rolled under the Honored tier (`menu_techniqueshow.mcfunction`). Granted `ds:limitless_technique`.
3. **Skill Progression**: Advancing the technique item via Sneak + Click (10 Invest Points per tier) sequentially unlocks:
   - Tier 1: `ds:limitless1` (Infinity & Spatial Dash)
   - Tier 2: `ds:limitless2` (Cursed Technique Lapse: Blue variants)
   - Tier 3: `ds:limitless3` (Cursed Technique Reversal: Red variants)
   - Tier 4: `ds:limitless4` (Spatial Blitz & Fury Barrage)
   - Tier 5: `ds:limitless5` (Imaginary Technique: Hollow Purple & 200% Purple)
   - Tier 6: `ds:limitless6` (Domain Expansion: Unlimited Void)
   - Combat: `ds:limitless_fists` (Blue-infused martial arts)
