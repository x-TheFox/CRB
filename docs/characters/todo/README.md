## 2. Canon Lore Summary
Aoi Todo is a powerhouse physical sorcerer possessing monstrous martial arts ability combined with an innate technique that disorients even the sharpest battle instincts. His technique, Boogie Woogie, allows him to instantly swap the positions of any two entities possessing cursed energy above a certain threshold with a single clap of his hands. Because a clap does not *guarantee* a swap, Todo can psychologically freeze opponents with fake claps. His tactical intellect operates at an imagined 530,000 IQ CPU speed, coordinating complex multi-party team swaps. In Shinjuku, Todo evolves Boogie Woogie into its vibraslap-mounted variant, increasing swap frequency to 50 times per second.
