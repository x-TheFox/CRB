# Aoi Todo — Canon Upgrades (§5.4)

## 1. Canon Abilities Missing or Truncated in JJA
1. **Shinjuku Showdown: Vibraslap Modification (高振動スラップ・50回/秒)**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapters 259–261 (Shinjuku Showdown vs Sukuna).
   - *Lore*: After losing his left hand to Mahito's Idle Transfiguration in Shibuya, Todo modifies his left arm with a musical vibraslap attached via binding vows. By altering the conditions of the technique to trigger upon the metal ball striking the wooden chamber, Todo increases his swap threshold to up to **50 swaps per second**, expanding his effective targeting radius and speed beyond comprehension.
   - *JJA omission*: JJA's Todo relies strictly on manual hand claps and has zero vibraslap content.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Equippable Cursed Arm Prosthetic: **The Vibraslap**.
     - Increases swap speed from 1.5s cooldown to 0.1s rapid pulse.
     - Allows "Continuous Spatial Flurry", relocating allies and enemies across dozens of coordinates in seconds to bypass Sukuna's perception.

2. **Expanded Multi-Target Dynamic Swapping**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 260. Todo swaps dozens of sorcerers, birds, and cursed energy tools instantaneously across the battlefield outside of Malevolent Shrine's radius.
   - *CRB Elevation*: Holding the clap key charges an area burst that swaps up to 6 entities simultaneously according to pre-selected priority filters.

3. **High-Handed Clap with Enemy Hand**:
   - *Canon ref*: Chapter 130. Todo claps Mahito's transfigured palm to activate Boogie Woogie even while his own palm is lacerated.
   - *CRB Elevation*: If Todo is grappled or in close melee contact with an enemy, he can trigger Boogie Woogie by slapping the opponent's palm or body.
