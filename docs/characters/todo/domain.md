# Aoi Todo — Domain Profile & Anti-Domain Arts

## 1. Domain Status
- **Innate Domain Expansion**: Todo possesses **no innate domain expansion** in canon or in JJA.
- **Anti-Domain Art: New Shadow Style: Simple Domain (シン・陰流 簡易領域)**:
  - *Canon ref*: Chapter 53 (against Hanami's domain), Chapter 129 (against Mahito's 0.2-second Self-Embodiment of Perfection).
  - *JJA implementation*: `functions/ce/simple_domain.mcfunction`.
  - *Trigger*: Anti-Domain Key (`B`).
  - *Cost / Upkeep*: 50 CE activation, 2 CE/s upkeep.
  - *Mechanics*:
    - Creates a 3-block diameter circular barrier around Todo's feet.
    - Completely strips the sure-hit property from any foreign domain barrier enclosing him.
    - Posture restriction: While standing inside the circle, physical defense increases by +20%, but moving more than 2 blocks outside breaks the barrier.
