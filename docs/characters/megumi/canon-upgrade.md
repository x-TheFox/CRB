# Megumi Fushiguro — Canon Completion & Upgrade Specification

## 1. Missing-Iconic Analysis (Canon Gap Identification)

### 1.1 The Well's Unknown Abyss (不知井底, Seitai Shirazu)
- **Canon Ref**: Chapter 44 / Episode 17
- **Gap in JJA**: JJA lacks combined fusion shikigami outside of Agito. The most iconic combination Megumi uses early in the series is **The Well's Unknown Abyss** (fusing Toad and Nue to create winged toads that don't suffer permanent death if destroyed).
- **Java Upgrade Spec**:
  - Ability: **The Well's Unknown Abyss**
  - Trigger: Combo select Toad + Nue.
  - Cost: 40 CE.
  - Effect: Spawns 3 winged toads that can fly and grapple enemies. Even if killed, neither Toad nor Nue is permanently lost.

### 1.2 Partial Manifestation Without Taming
- **Canon Ref**: Chapter 218 (Sukuna's use of Ten Shadows)
- **Gap in JJA**: In JJA, untamed shikigami can only be fought in rigid ritual arenas. Canonically, an advanced user can partially manifest the properties or limbs of an untamed or tamed shikigami (e.g. projecting Max Elephant's water directly from one's hands, or using Nue's wings for gliding) without summoning the full physical creature.
- **Java Upgrade Spec**:
  - Ability: **Shadow Partial Manifestation**
  - Effect: Allows gliding using Nue's wings, or shooting a water jet directly from palms using Max Elephant's water reserve.

### 1.3 Carrying Allies in Shadow Inventory
- **Canon Ref**: Chapter 214
- **Gap in JJA**: JJA only permits storing items in shadow storage. Canonically, Megumi can submerge living allies (such as Yuji or Maki) into his shadow to transport them covertly!
- **Java Upgrade Spec**:
  - Megumi can hold sneak and interact with a willing player to submerge them into his shadow for up to 30 seconds.

## 2. Fidelity Corrections
1. **Chimera Shadow Garden Barrier Logic**: In JJA, the domain is often treated as a standard enclosed sphere. In canon (Chapters 58, 170), Megumi's domain is explicitly incomplete and requires physical structural enclosures to function at full capacity. In CRB, this architectural restriction is implemented faithfully.

- **Judgment Call: Mahoraga Ritual in Multiplayer**:
