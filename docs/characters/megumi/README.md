# Character Dossier: Megumi Fushiguro (伏黒 恵)

## 1. Identity & Technique Profile
- **Character**: Megumi Fushiguro — Zenin Clan Heir, Grade 2 Jujutsu Sorcerer
- **Innate Technique**: **The Ten Shadows Technique (十種影法術, Tokusa no Kage Bōjutsu)**
- **Domain Expansion**: **Chimera Shadow Garden (嵌合暗翳庭, Kangō An'eitei)**
- **Ultimate Shikigami**: **Eight-Handled Sword Divergent Sila Divine General Mahoraga (八握剣異戒神将魔虚羅)**
- **JJA Internal Namespace / Folders**: `functions/shikigami/`, `items/10shadows/`, tags `shikigami`, `zenin`

## 2. Canon Lore Summary
Megumi Fushiguro is the inheritor of the Zenin Clan's most prized hereditary cursed technique: the Ten Shadows. Using shadows as an intermediary, Megumi can summon up to ten distinct shikigami by forming specific shadow-puppet hand signs. The technique operates under strict rules of succession: if a shikigami is permanently destroyed, its power and technique are inherited by the surviving shikigami (such as Divine Dog: Totality). The crowning jewel of the Ten Shadows is the untamed Divine General Mahoraga, capable of adapting to any and all phenomena through the turning of its eight-handled wheel. In addition to summons, Megumi can hide within shadows and store cursed tools inside his shadow inventory.

## 3. Progression & Unlock Arc in JJA
1. **Clan Rolling**: Zenin Clan (6% chance) grants eligibility for the Honored tier Ten Shadows Technique (`ds:10shadows_technique`).
2. **Untamed Taming Arc**: In JJA, all shikigami start untamed. Players must initiate ritual subjugation battles against each shadow to unlock them (`functions/shikigami/shikigami1.mcfunction` through `shikigami10.mcfunction`).
3. **Shadow Wheel & Mahoraga**: Subjugating or invoking Mahoraga places the Eight-Handled Wheel above the caster's head (`ds:mahoragawheel`), which tracks adaptation ticks against active damage types.
4. **Domain Expansion**: Advancing to Tier 12 unlocks `ds:shikigami12` (Chimera Shadow Garden).
